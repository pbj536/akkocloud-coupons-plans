15% off, all plans, and reviewers report it keeps working on renewals. That second part is the whole reason to keep the code saved. A first-order discount saves you money once; a recurring one changes what the server costs you every year you keep it. On the ¥299 plan it takes ¥44.85 off, which is ¥254.15.

Set a reminder for the renewal invoice either way. Discount codes and renewal pricing can change, and no source reviewed here publishes the undiscounted renewal rate, so the only reliable check is your own client area at invoice time.

### NEW2026: 20% off, first order only

New customers, one use, 20% off. On the ¥299 plan that's ¥239.20, about ¥15 cheaper than AKKO15 in year one and nothing in year two. If it's your first order, use this one and switch to AKKO15 when the renewal comes up.

Every plan in this article is a VPS, so VPS20 sits in the same territory as the other two. Where two codes both apply, enter each one and compare the cart total. Nothing in the sources says whether codes stack, so assume one code per order and don't plan around a combined discount.

## What the CN2 GIA line buys you

All AkkoCloud plans route return traffic over CN2 GIA, China Telecom's premium international path through the 59.43 nodes, including the ¥299 tier. Third-party latency figures put San Jose at about 154ms for Telecom users in one review and about 168ms in another (Shanghai fastest at 125ms, Baoding slowest at 218ms), Frankfurt around 180ms against roughly 230ms for ordinary European VPS, London around 200ms, and Hong Kong in the 10–30ms range. Hardware is AMD EPYC with SSD in RAID10, Geekbench 6 single-core around 1,100–1,200, and the storage is SSD rather than NVMe.

Some IPs are native and users report unlocking Netflix and similar services. AkkoCloud doesn't advertise that, and the San Jose product page explicitly warns that the test IP doesn't guarantee your assigned IP belongs to the same range. Buy for latency and routing, not for streaming.

## Four things to know before you pay

**No refunds.** AkkoCloud's own product page states that the terms of service don't allow refunds, and a review of the billing process says the same. This is the single most important fact in this article. There is no money-back window, no trial, no pro-rata exit. If you're unsure, monthly billing at ¥50 is your insurance.

**Alipay only.** Payment is Alipay, settled in RMB. No credit card, no PayPal, no WeChat Pay. Convenient if you're in mainland China, a hard stop if you're not.

**Unmanaged.** You patch the OS and update the software. AkkoCloud hands you a KVM instance with a control panel for reinstalls, reboots and traffic monitoring, plus a choice of Linux distributions and Windows Server.

**Support runs on a schedule.** Tickets plus QQ and a Telegram group, staffed 12:00–22:00 China time, with a stated commitment of a 24-hour reply and 48-hour resolution for issues filed during those hours. Problems filed overnight wait for the next shift. Users report IPs getting blocked and replaced free after contacting support, which is the case where the ticket queue matters most.

## Who shouldn't buy this

Skip AkkoCloud if you need to pay by credit card or PayPal, if a refund window is part of how you buy hosting, if you want a managed server with someone else applying security patches, or if you need 8GB of RAM on a published price list rather than a custom quote. Those are four different reasons to look elsewhere, and none of them are fixed by a coupon.
